# Settings

Changes how the desktop looks and behaves. Every change takes effect straight away, and is
saved to your account, so it follows you to any computer you sign in from.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: *System* follows your browser's light or dark setting. *Light* and *Dark* pick one.
- **Background**:
  - *Automatic* shows the photo when your connection is good, and drawn shapes otherwise.
  - *Photo* always shows the photo, which uses more data.
  - *Plain* always shows the drawn shapes, which use no data.
  - *My picture* shows a picture from your files. Type its path, such as
    `/home/your-name/beach.jpg`, and press **Use**.

## Performance

- **Reduce motion** turns animations off. Things snap into place instead of sliding.
- **Save data** never downloads a background picture, and tells apps to load less. Apps look
  plainer, but the desktop uses less of the network.

## Dock

Tick an app to pin it to the dock. A pinned app stays there even when it isn't open, so you
can start it with one click. If it has several windows open, each click brings the next one
forward. Untick it to take it off.

## Account

**Switch account** signs you out, so you or someone else can sign in with a different account.

If the server has login turned off, there are no accounts. Your settings are then kept in the
browser you are using, not on the server.
