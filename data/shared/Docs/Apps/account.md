# Account

Shows who you are signed in as, and lets you make app passwords for programs that cannot sign
in through the browser.

- **Manage account** opens your account page in a new tab, where you can change your password
  and your details.
- **Sign out** ends your session. The desktop goes back to the sign-in page.

## App passwords

An app password lets a program that cannot use the sign-in page, such as a network drive
(see [Your files as a drive](../WebDAV.md)), sign in as you. Each one is for a single device,
and works with your user name instead of your real password.

- Type a name for the device, such as `Laptop`, and press **Create**. The password is shown
  **once**: copy it into the program then, and press **Done**.
- Each one in the list shows when you made it and when it was last used.
- **Revoke** one when you stop using that device, or if it is lost. It stops working at once,
  and your other devices are not affected.

You can have up to 20 at a time.

If the server has login turned off, the app says so: everyone on the network gets in, and
nobody has a private folder.
